# v1.1 — iOS/iPadOS 17+ Compatibility, Regular Capture, 16-bit Pipeline & Camera Acceleration

## 1. Earliest iOS and iPadOS Deployment Target: **17.0**

Compiler probes across the full module (`-IDEBuildingContinueBuildingAfterErrors=YES`) establish a sharp architectural boundary at **iOS 17.0 / iPadOS 17.0** (matching Xcode 27.2’s `RECOMMENDED_IPHONEOS_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET = 17.0`):

| Deployment target | Unique unavailable APIs | Architectural impact |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **iOS / iPadOS 26.0** (v1.0) | 0 | Baseline shipped in v1.0. |
| **iOS / iPadOS 18.0** | 14 (48 sites) | Liquid Glass (`GlassEffectContainer`, `.glassEffect`, `.glass` / `.glassProminent`), `ToolbarSpacer`, `.sharedBackgroundVisibility`, `MKReverseGeocodingRequest`, `nominalFocalLengthIn35mmFilm`, `UISlider.TrackConfiguration`, `interactiveContentPopGestureRecognizer`, `settingContentHeadroom` / `calculatedContentHeadroom`. |
| **iOS / iPadOS 17.0** | 26 (71 sites) | Adds only 5 localized iOS 18 features: `MTLCompileOptions.mathMode` (replaces `fastMathEnabled = false`), Camera Control (`AVCaptureSystemExposureBiasSlider`), `.onCameraCaptureEvent`, 2-arg `.defaultScrollAnchor(_:for:)` (1-arg `.defaultScrollAnchor(_:)` is iOS 17.0), and `contentHeadroom` / `CIToneMapHeadroom`. |
| **iOS / iPadOS 16.0** | 105+ | Breaks core foundations: `@Observable` / `Observation` (7 models), `SwiftData` (`@Model`, `VersionedSchema`, `SchemaMigrationPlan`), `MPSGraphFFTDescriptor` (`realToHermiteanFFT` for optical diffusion), `AVCaptureDevice.RotationCoordinator`, `AVCapturePhotoOutput.captureReadiness` / `isResponsiveCaptureSupported`, and SwiftUI `ContentUnavailableView` / `ScrollPosition`. |

### Device Coverage Gained at iOS / iPadOS 17.0
- **iPhone**: Adds **iPhone XS, XS Max, XR (A12)**, **iPhone SE (2nd & 3rd gen)**, and **iPhone 11–16** devices still running iOS 17 or iOS 18.
- **iPad**: Adds **iPad (6th–10th gen, A10+)**, **iPad Pro 10.5" & 12.9" (2nd gen, A10X+)**, **iPad Air (3rd gen+)**, and **iPad mini (5th gen+)**.

### Key Fallbacks Implemented
- **Liquid Glass & Toolbars (`PlatformCompatibility.swift`)**: Preserves native Liquid Glass and `ToolbarSpacer` on iOS 26+; falls back cleanly to `.regularMaterial`, `.bordered` / `.borderedProminent`, and flexible `Spacer()` bottom-bar items on iOS 17–18.
- **Bipolar Slider Fill (`ParameterControl.swift`)**: Uses `UISlider.TrackConfiguration` on iOS 26+; renders an identical neutral-origin accent track bar inside `PrecisionSlider` below the thumb on iOS 17–18, keeping center-zero EV/balance sliders consistent.
- **35mm Equivalent Lens Focal Length (`CameraService.swift`)**: Uses `nominalFocalLengthIn35mmFilm` on iOS 26+; derives exact diagonal 35mm focal length from `photoFormat.videoFieldOfView` and sensor aspect ratio (`0.5 * hypot(36, 24) * (w / hypot(w, h)) / tan(fov / 2)`) on iOS 17–18.
- **Reverse Geocoding (`PhotoDetailView.swift`)**: Uses `MKReverseGeocodingRequest` on iOS 26+ and `CLGeocoder` with cancellation on iOS 17–18.
- **Metal Precision (`MetalDispatch.swift`)**: Uses `mathMode = .safe` + `mathFloatingPointFunctions = .precise` on iOS 18+ and `fastMathEnabled = false` on iOS 17.

## 2. Regular (HEIC / JPEG) Camera Capture for Non-RAW Devices and Lenses

- `CameraFormat` now supports `.proRAW`, `.bayerRAW`, `.heic`, and `.jpeg`.
- Non-RAW phones and iPads (and non-RAW secondary lenses such as front cameras or older ultrawide cameras) open directly in `.ready` mode using `HEIC` (or `JPEG` if HEVC is unavailable) instead of failing with `RAW Capture Unavailable`.
- High-resolution processed capture (`12 MP` vs `Max`, e.g., 24 MP / 48 MP HEIC on non-Pro iPhone 15/16) is supported whenever `supportedMaxPhotoDimensions` exceeds 12 MP.
- RAW-capable phones retain `.proRAW` (or `.bayerRAW`) as default, also expose `HEIC` in the format menu for fast lightweight shooting, and remember the user's preferred RAW format across temporary flips to non-RAW lenses.
- `CameraInbox` accepts `.dng`, `.heic`, `.jpg`, and `.jpeg` captures with full EXIF and optional GPS metadata preservation.

## 3. Rendering Bit Depth on Older Devices: Why **16-bit Float** (and Not 8-bit)

### Why Going Below 16-bit Float Is Unsound
- An **8-bit integer (`RGBA8`)** or **10-bit fixed-point** working pipeline clamps scene-linear light at `1.0`, destroying RAW/HDR highlight headroom before film shoulder compression, halation highlight boost, and post-film Lift/Gain highlight recovery, while quantizing deep linear shadows to a handful of discrete codes.

### Why Hybrid **16-bit IEEE Half-Float (`RGBAh` / `.rgba16Float`)** Is Ideal
- IEEE 754 `binary16` spans `±65,504` (~16 stops above diffuse white `1.0`) with 1,024 steps per stop of exposure — matching Core Image’s native default working format while halving memory footprint and GPU texture bandwidth (`8 bytes/pixel` vs `16 bytes/pixel`).
- **Internal photochemical stability preserved**: Inside `MetalFilmPipeline`, per-frame/per-tile buffers keep 32-bit `Float` for 4th-order recursive IIR Gaussian blurs (`iir_pass`), `MPSGraph` 2D FFT diffusion, and Philox/Poisson grain so recursive filters cannot accumulate rounding drift.
- **50% reduction on retained & tiled working storage**: Core Image working contexts (`SourceImageRenderer`, `PhotoColorBridge`, `ToneRenderer`, `FilmEdgeRenderer`), retained preview working `CGImage`s, `ToneRenderer` 2D Metal textures (`input`, `output`, `residualTextures`), and file-backed `PixelStore` (`.half`) switch to 16-bit float.
- **Settings & Automatic Policy**:
  - Added **Rendering pipeline** (`Automatic`, `32-bit float`, `16-bit float`) in **About → Settings** (`about.renderPrecision`).
  - `Automatic` selects `16-bit float` on iOS devices under ~5.5 GB RAM (all 2–4 GB iPhones and iPads) and `32-bit float` on 6 GB+ devices.
  - Unset `PreviewQuality` defaults to `1080 px` on devices under 4.5 GB RAM (`2160 px` otherwise), and `MetalFilmPipeline.Tiling` adds a `768 px` tile tier when available process memory drops below `650 MB`.

## 4. Camera Capture Latency Root Causes & Pipeline Accelerations

| Bottleneck in v1.0 | Fix in v1.1 |
| --- | --- |
| **Shutter & UI locked for entire ISP fusion + disk write**: `CameraModel.takePhoto()` held `busy = true` and `CameraService` enforced `capture == nil` until `didFinishCaptureFor` (1.5–4.5 s on ProRAW), defeating `isResponsiveCaptureEnabled`. | **Two-phase overlapping capture**: `takePhoto` returns on `didCapturePhotoFor` (unlocking shutter, EV, Close, and Library immediately after sensor exposure, ~80–200 ms) while `captures[uniqueID]` overlaps ISP processing in the background and drains safely on close/lens switch. |
| **Synchronous 25–85 MB DNG serialization & `.atomic` write on AVFoundation’s callback thread**. | Offloaded `CameraInbox.store` onto dedicated serial `SpektraCam.CameraWrite` queue (`qos: .userInitiated`). |
| **Uncompressed LPCM ProRAW DNGs (~75–85 MB at 48 MP)**. | Enabled mathematically **lossless JPEG-XL ProRAW** (`AVVideoCodecType.JPEGXL`, `AVVideoQualityKey: 1.0`) on iOS 18+ hardware where supported, cutting DNG size and disk I/O by ~55–65%. |
| **Cold startup probed all 4 cameras sequentially**, creating 4 `AVCaptureDeviceInput`s and toggling `isAppleProRAWEnabled` repeatedly before `startRunning()`. | Startup attaches **only** the initial primary rear camera and configures `isAppleProRAWEnabled` once before `startRunning()`; secondary inputs initialize lazily on lens switch. |
| **`preparedPhotoSettingsArray` prepared all formats with `flash: false`**, causing on-the-fly ISP reconfiguration when flash or format changed, and `rawPixelFormats` kept the last pixel format instead of AVFoundation’s preferred first format. | Keeps the first matching pixel format and synchronizes `setPreparedPhotoSettingsArray` with the active `format` + `flash` state. |
| **Redundant GPU `CIRAWFilter` demosaic during capture import**: `LibraryFiles.importPhoto` ran a full GPU RAW decode just to build a 384 px thumbnail, contending with the live camera viewfinder and ISP. | `PhotoSource.embeddedThumbnail` decodes the embedded 1080 px RAW thumbnail on CPU (`kCGImageSourceCreateThumbnailFromImageIfAbsent: false`, aspect- and size-validated) in ~2 ms with zero GPU contention; Viewer and Editor still decode native RAW. |
